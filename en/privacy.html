2. What data we receive

We receive only what you enter on the site yourself, and the technical data the site and its forms cannot work without.

  • Request form: name, email address, phone or messenger handle (optional), the areas you selected, the description of your task, the page language and the time you ticked your consent to this policy.
  • Newsletter: email address. The subscription starts once you confirm it through the link in our email.
  • Correspondence: what you write to us by email or phone, including files. Files (for example, a letter from the tax office) may contain tax and financial details and data about other people — send only what we need to reply.
  • Technical data: when you open a page, the site's host (GitHub Pages) receives your IP address and browser details and may record them in security logs. When you send a request, the same is received by Cloudflare, which passes the request on to Brevo; the Cloudflare request log records the IP address, browser details and approximate location derived from the IP. When you subscribe to the newsletter, Brevo receives your IP address and browser details.

The site has no analytics, advertising, cookies or third-party fonts or scripts: every file loads from lmedia.it itself.

5. How long we keep it

  • Requests without a contract — 12 months from the last contact, then deleted.
  • Once a contract is signed — as long as the law requires (accounting records — 10 years, Art. 2220 c.c.).
  • Newsletter — until you unsubscribe.
  • To limit the number of requests per day we keep not your IP address itself but a hash of it combined with the date — for 2 days; the Cloudflare counter (no more than 3 requests a minute) holds the address for about a minute.
  • Cloudflare request log — up to 7 days; host logs — under GitHub's rules (GitHub does not publish the period).

6. Your rights

At any time you can ask for access to your data, its correction, deletion, restriction of processing and transfer in a convenient format, and you can object to processing and withdraw consent (Art. 15–22 GDPR). Write to info@lmedia.it or PEC lmedia@pec.it — we reply within a month.

If you believe we are breaching your rights, you can complain to the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (www.garanteprivacy.it).

7. Cookies

The site uses no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. Fonts and images load from lmedia.it, with no third-party services. If we add analytics or other services that use cookies, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.

8. Children and changes

The site is not intended for people under 14 (Art. 2-quinquies D.lgs. 196/2003). We will announce significant changes to this policy on this page.
