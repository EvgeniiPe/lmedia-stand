Services

What we handle

We take on what you need now. Everything else we add when it's needed, and you won't have to change providers.

Legal support

The lawyer handles contracts, registration and disputes and explains them in English.

  • Pre-start check: what your residence status allows
  • Business form: sole proprietorship (ditta individuale) or limited company (SRL, SRLS)
  • Contracts with clients, suppliers and partners
  • Premises lease and notice of opening (SCIA)
  • Claims and disputes with clients and suppliers

Accounting and taxes

Partita IVA, tax regime, bookkeeping and payments on the tax calendar.

  • Opening a partita IVA and an ATECO activity code
  • Flat-rate forfettario regime or the standard one: we calculate on your figures
  • Bookkeeping and electronic invoices (fattura elettronica)
  • Tax returns, VAT and F24 payments (the single tax payment form)
  • Replies to the tax office and INPS, disputing debts

Marketing and communication

Brand, website and press in Italian, so local clients can find and understand you.

  • Positioning: what to say about your business and to whom
  • Name, brand and copy in Italian
  • A website in Italian that people find on Google
  • Press, client reviews, a company profile on Google Maps
  • Checking “green” claims in advertising (EU rule from 27.09.2026)

FAQ

Frequently asked
questions

What people ask before the first call.

Ask your question
Is the first call paid?

No. 20 minutes, free and with no obligation. At the end we tell you what to do next and which of us you need. If you don't need us, we'll say so.

Do you only work in Florence?

Our office is in Florence; we handle accounting and contracts for clients all over Italy.

What language will the documents be in?

Official documents are in Italian. We explain and correspond in English.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the task. After the call we send a written proposal with a price; work starts only after your “yes”.

Can I start with just one area?

Yes. For example, accounting only. We'll bring in the lawyer and the marketer when they're needed.

Can a debt to the tax office or INPS be reduced?

Yes, if the debt was charged in error or the limitation period has expired. We check the calculation and the deadlines, dispute wrongful charges and, if needed, arrange an instalment plan. Whether the debt can be reduced, and by how much, becomes clear after we review the documents. The deadline to appeal is short, so send us the demand as soon as it arrives.
